Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at Université de Sherbrooke.

My research interests include macroeconomics, structural change, international trade, and development economics.

I study how economies transform as they develop: how trade integration, land and labor market frictions, and changing input-output linkages shape the reallocation of resources across sectors. I also work on production theory and productivity measurement. See my papers.

I have experience with policy institutions. Since September 2021, I have been at the World Bank.
